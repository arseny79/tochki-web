Tochki Club

A school-notebook classic. No ads, and it works offline.

  • Play against a bot — three levels: Easy, Medium, Hard — or with a friend on one device.
  • Boards 9×9, 13×13 and 17×17.
  • Free, with no ads and no purchases in the first version.
  • No sign-up.

How to play

  1. Place dots in turns on the grid intersections, like on squared notebook paper. Blue moves first.
  2. Enclose your opponent's dots with a ring of your own — enclosed dots are captured.
  3. Score: 1 point for each captured dot. The higher score wins.
